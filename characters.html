Main Characters

Explore the iconic heroes and villains

☣ Resident Evil 0 (2002)

Rebecca Chambers

The youngest S.T.A.R.S. Bravo Team member and skilled medic, forced to team up with a convicted criminal to survive the Umbrella Training Facility.

Billy Coen

A former Marine wrongfully convicted of murder, who escapes his transport and teams up with Rebecca to survive the Arklay Mountains outbreak.

☣ Resident Evil 1 (1996)

Chris Redfield

S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team member investigating the Spencer Mansion.

Jill Valentine

S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team member and master of unlocking.

Barry Burton

A reliable S.T.A.R.S. member and weapons expert.

Albert Wesker

S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team captain hiding a dark secret.

☣ Resident Evil 2 (1998)

Leon S. Kennedy

A rookie RPD officer surviving his first day on the job.

Claire Redfield

Searching for her brother Chris in zombie-infested Raccoon City.

Ada Wong

A mysterious spy helping Leon while pursuing her own agenda.

William Birkin

An Umbrella scientist who injected himself with the G-virus.

Mr. X (Tyrant)

A relentless Tyrant deployed by Umbrella to retrieve the G-virus sample and eliminate all survivors in Raccoon City Police Department.

☣ Resident Evil 3 (1999)

Jill Valentine

A S.T.A.R.S. survivor trying to escape Raccoon City.

Carlos Oliveira

A mercenary who helps Jill escape from Raccoon City.

Nemesis

A relentless bio-weapon programmed to eliminate S.T.A.R.S. members.

☣ Resident Evil 4 (2005)

Leon S. Kennedy

A government agent rescuing the President's daughter in Spain.

Ada Wong

A spy working in the shadows with her own mission.

Ashley Graham

The US President's daughter kidnapped by a mysterious cult.

Luis Serra

A former Umbrella scientist seeking redemption.

Jack Krauser

A soldier corrupted by power and betrayal.

Albert Wesker

Operating from the shadows, Wesker manipulates Los Illuminados cult to obtain a Plaga sample for his own sinister experiments.

☣ Resident Evil 5 (2009)

Chris Redfield

A BSAA agent investigating bioterrorism in Africa.

Sheva Alomar

A skilled BSAA agent and Chris Redfield's partner in Africa.

Jill Valentine

Presumed dead after a confrontation with Wesker, Jill was captured and used as a test subject for Uroboros virus, becoming a brainwashed weapon under Wesker's control.

Albert Wesker

The main antagonist seeking to reshape humanity with Uroboros.

☣ Resident Evil 6 (2012)

Leon S. Kennedy

A government agent tasked with stopping a bioterror attack on the US President.

Helena Harper

Leon's partner who carries a dark secret connected to the Tall Oaks bioterror attack.

Chris Redfield

A BSAA captain haunted by memories of losing his squad to a bioterror attack in Edonia.

Piers Nivans

Chris Redfield's loyal BSAA partner who sacrifices himself to save Chris from the C-virus.

Jake Muller

Albert Wesker's son whose blood holds the key to a vaccine against the C-virus.

Sherry Birkin

William Birkin's daughter now working as a government agent, partnered with Jake Muller.

Ada Wong

Framed by her doppelganger Carla Radames, Ada fights to clear her name and stop the C-virus.

☣ Resident Evil 7 Biohazard (2017)

Ethan Winters

An ordinary man who travels to Louisiana to find his missing wife Mia, only to be trapped by the Baker family.

Mia Winters

Ethan's wife found alive after three years, hiding a dark secret connected to Eveline.

Jack Baker

The infected patriarch of the Baker family, transformed into a near-immortal monster by Eveline.

Marguerite Baker

Jack's infected wife who controls swarms of insects and mutated plants throughout the Baker estate.

Lucas Baker

Jack's sadistic son who sets deadly traps for Ethan, secretly working with a mysterious organization.

Zoe Baker

Jack's daughter who resists Eveline's control and secretly guides Ethan to escape the Baker estate.

Eveline

A dangerous bio-weapon disguised as a little girl, capable of mind control and infecting entire families.

Chris Redfield

A mysterious soldier who arrives at the Baker estate to rescue Mia and neutralize the Eveline threat.

☣ Resident Evil Village (2021)

Ethan Winters

A father fighting through a mysterious European village to save his kidnapped daughter Rose from the Four Lords.

Mia Winters

Ethan's wife whose fate takes a dark turn when Mother Miranda infiltrates the Winters household.

Chris Redfield

A seasoned BSAA operative who has been tracking Mother Miranda and her connections to the Mold bioweapon.

Rose Winters

Ethan's infant daughter kidnapped by Mother Miranda and separated into four flasks across the village.

Mother Miranda

The cult leader and main antagonist who rules the village, seeking to resurrect her dead daughter Eva using Rose.

Lady Dimitrescu

A 9-foot tall vampire countess ruling Castle Dimitrescu with her three daughters, loyal to Mother Miranda.

Karl Heisenberg

A lord with magnetic powers who controls metal and machinery, secretly plotting against Mother Miranda.

Donna Beneviento

A reclusive lord who uses her doll Angie and hallucinogenic flowers to torment her victims in House Beneviento.

Salvatore Moreau

A grotesque fish-like lord who controls the reservoir, desperately seeking Mother Miranda's approval.

☣ Resident Evil Requiem (2026)

Grace Ashcroft

An FBI analyst investigating mysterious deaths at the Wrenwood Hotel, where her mother Alyssa was murdered eight years ago.

Leon S. Kennedy

A veteran DSO agent returning to the ruins of Raccoon City, older and wearier but still fighting bioterrorism 28 years later.

Dr. Viktor Gideon

The main antagonist of Requiem, a virologist with ties to Umbrella Corporation secretly developing a dangerous new virus codenamed Elpis.

Alyssa Ashcroft

Grace's mother and a journalist who survived Raccoon City. Murdered at the Wrenwood Hotel after uncovering Gideon's secret experiments.

Sherry Birkin

William Birkin's daughter, now a government agent serving as Leon's sidekick during the Raccoon City investigation in Requiem.

Zeno

A mysterious and dangerous figure connected to Gideon's experiments who summons Mr. X to eliminate Leon throughout Requiem.

HUNK

The legendary Umbrella mercenary known as "Mr. Death" who returns in Requiem, confronting Leon in a late-game melee encounter inside Raccoon City.

Mr. X (Super Tyrant)

A mass-produced Tyrant variant summoned by Zeno to hunt Leon through the ruins of Raccoon City Police Department, 28 years after the original incident.
